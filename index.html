Reading, made easier

Di-Friend is a Safari extension for people with dyslexia and anyone who finds reading on screens tiring. A friendly font, a warm background, a focus window and read aloud — on any website.

Coming soon to the App Store

7 days free, then $0.99 per month or $9.99 per year · iPhone, iPad and Mac · no ads, no data collection

Easy-to-read font

Comic Sans or the similar Comic Relief — letters with distinct shapes are easier to tell apart. Rounded Varela Round for Hebrew. Add more space between letters, words and lines.

Warm background

Beige, cream, peach, rose or sand instead of glaring white. Dark websites are left untouched.

Focus window

A bright strip for one or more lines; everything else is softly dimmed and blurred. Text scrolls under the strip — drag it to move, pull the edge to resize.

Read aloud

Listen to the selected text, the page from any point, or what’s in the focus window. English, Russian and Hebrew in your device’s voices, at your speed.

How to install

  1. Download Di-Friend from the App Store, open the app and start the free trial.
  2. Tap Turn on in Safari and switch on Di-Friend.
  3. Under Permissions choose All Websites → Allow.
  4. Open any website in Safari — a round Di-Friend button appears in the bottom right.
