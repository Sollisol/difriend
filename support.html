Turning it on — iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the Di-Friend app and tap Turn on in Safari.
    No such button (iOS below 26.2)? Go to Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Di-Friend. On iOS 17 and earlier: Settings → Safari → Extensions.
  2. Switch on Di-Friend.
  3. Under Permissions choose All Websites → Allow.
  4. Go back to Safari and reload the page.

Turning it on — Mac

  1. Open the Di-Friend app and click Open Safari Settings.
  2. Tick Di-Friend.
  3. Click Edit Websites… and set Other Websites to Allow.

Common questions

The Di-Friend button doesn’t appear

  • Reload the page: the extension doesn’t enter pages that were open before it was turned on.
  • Check that access to all websites is allowed (see above). If it’s set to “Ask”, Safari waits for permission on every site.
  • Check that the button is on: Di-Friend icon in Safari’s extensions menu → “Di-Friend button on pages”.
  • Extensions don’t work on Safari’s own pages, in the App Store or in PDF files — that’s how Safari works.

How to open the settings

iPhone and iPad: in the Safari address bar tap the page icon (or aA) → Di-Friend. Mac: the Di-Friend icon in the Safari toolbar. The language (English / Russian) is switched at the top of the settings.

Read aloud is silent or the voice sounds odd

  • Check that your iPhone isn’t in silent mode.
  • Download a better voice: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, Russian or Hebrew.
  • On iPhone and iPad, start reading from the round button on the page.

The background didn’t change

Di-Friend deliberately leaves dark websites alone. If a light site didn’t change, try the “Replace background” mode in the settings.

Using the focus window

Turn it on in the settings or with the button on the page. Scroll the text — the strip stays in place. The right handle moves the strip, the handle on the bottom edge changes its height. On Mac: ⌥↓ / ⌥↑ scrolls by exactly one window height.

Subscription

  • The first 7 days are free, then $0.99 per month or $9.99 per year. One subscription works on iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple ID.
  • To cancel: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Di-Friend. If you cancel during the free trial, you won’t be charged.
  • New device or reinstall: open the Di-Friend app and tap “Restore purchases”.
  • Subscribed, but websites still show “Start free trial”? Reload the page in Safari.

A website’s layout broke

Remove the line spacing in the settings or temporarily turn Di-Friend off with the main switch. And send us the website address — we’ll fix it.
