Di-Friend Support Privacy RU

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

In short

Di-Friend does not collect, store on its own servers or share any data about you. There are no servers, accounts, analytics or ads.

What the extension does on websites

To change the font and background and to show the focus window, the extension needs access to the pages you open in Safari. All of this happens on your device. Page content, website addresses and browsing history are never sent anywhere or saved.

Read aloud

Text is read by your device’s built-in voices (iPhone, iPad or Mac). The text being read never leaves your device.

Settings

Your chosen background colour, spacing, focus window height, reading speed and language are kept in Safari’s extension storage on your device. If Safari syncs extension data between your devices via iCloud, Apple does this within your account — we have no access to that data.

Subscription

The subscription is purchased and paid for through the App Store. Payment details are handled by Apple and are not available to us. The app checks whether your subscription is active using Apple’s tools on your device and stores only the fact “subscription active until a certain date”, so that the Safari extension knows it can work.

Fonts

The Comic Relief and Varela Round fonts are bundled with the extension and are not downloaded from the internet.

Third parties

Di-Friend does not use third-party services, SDKs, trackers or ad networks.

Children

Di-Friend can be used at any age: it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future feature needs data (for example, cloud voices), we will update this page in advance and ask for your consent in the app.
